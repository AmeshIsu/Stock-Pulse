## 1. What Stock Pulse Does (and Does Not Do)

### What Stock Pulse Does:
- **Ranks Stocks Cross-Sectionally**: Every trading day, Stock Pulse evaluates the constituents of the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) S&P SL20 index and ranks them relative to one another based on upward potential.
- **Multi-Horizon Perspective**: Provides separate predictive rankings across three time horizons:
  - **Short Horizon (21 Trading Days)**: Tactical ~1-month momentum and mean-reversion.
  - **Mid Horizon (63 Trading Days)**: Quarterly ~3-month trend-following.
  - **Long Horizon (252 Trading Days)**: Strategic ~1-year capital appreciation potential.
- **Categorizes Stocks into Actionable Tiers**: Classifies ranked companies into **High Upside**, **Neutral**, and **Low Upside** tiers, alongside volatility risk buckets and liquidity warning flags.
- **Institutional Rigor**: Enforces zero future leakage, purge-and-embargo validation splits, and accounts for realistic frontier market trading costs (100 basis points per side).

### What Stock Pulse Does NOT Do:
- It does **not** predict absolute price targets (e.g., 'Stock X will reach LKR 150').
- It does **not** incorporate macroeconomic news headlines, quarterly financial statements, corporate earnings disclosures, or qualitative management assessments.
- It does **not** guarantee trading profits or downside capital protection during systemic market crashes.

## 2. The Data & Ranking Universe

- **Index Membership**: Operates on the 20 member companies of the Colombo Stock Exchange **S&P SL20** index.
- **Share Class Selection**: Evaluates the primary voting ordinary share class (`.N`) for all companies, except for Seylan Bank where the liquid non-voting ordinary share line (`SEYB.X`) is selected due to superior liquidity and data history (`universe_selection: most_rows_among_N_X`).
- **Historical Depth**: Spans 24 calendar years of official CSE daily market trading records (2001 through 2025).
- **Data As-Of Date**: The application interface displays the latest effective closing trade date evaluated by the engine.

## 3. How Upside Scores Are Built

Every stock's served upside score is a cross-sectional percentile (from 0 to 100) computed each day relative to its peers:
- A score of **100** represents the highest upside potential in the index.
- A score of **50** represents the median stock.
- A score of **0** represents the lowest upside potential.

### Model Candidates & Serving Governance
Scores are generated by institutional-grade quantitative algorithms selected via strict out-of-sample walk-forward validation:
- **Short Horizon (21d)**: Served by **`ens_rank4`** (a four-model rank ensemble combining regularized Ridge regression, Random Forest, and LightGBM).
- **Mid Horizon (63d)**: Served by **`momentum_126`** (classical 6-month cross-sectional price momentum).
- **Long Horizon (252d)**: Served by **`ens_rank4`** (four-model rank ensemble).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deep Learning Evaluation Note**: Advanced deep-learning architectures (LSTM recurrent rankers and TabPFN in-context foundation models) were rigorously evaluated for research completeness. However, they are **not** deployed in the live scoring pipeline because they require heavy retraining overhead, consume substantial compute, and demonstrated no reliable advantage over classical momentum and tree-based gradient boosting on this 20-stock universe.

### Overall Upside Score Calculation
The overall upside score displayed on company summary cards is a weighted average of the three horizon percentiles. The default weights are mathematically proportional to the statistical evidence points established during validation (RED = 1, AMBER = 2, GREEN = 3):
- **Short Horizon**: `25.0%` (RED: 1 pt / 4 total)
- **Mid Horizon**: `50.0%` (AMBER: 2 pts / 4 total)
- **Long Horizon**: `25.0%` (RED: 1 pt / 4 total)
*Users can adjust these horizon weights interactively in the web application based on their personalized investment horizon.*

## 4. How We Tested It (Validation Standards)

Stock Pulse was tested using institutional walk-forward out-of-sample backtesting:
1. **Walk-Forward Annual Expanding Folds**: The model trains only on historical data up to year $T-1$, and predicts exclusively on out-of-sample year $T$. Models never see future data.
2. **Purging & Embargoing**: 21 to 252 days of trailing historical training rows are purged before each test fold so forward target windows never overlap test periods. A 5-day post-training embargo prevents boundary leakage.
3. **Block-Resampled Random Null Band**: Every model is tested against a persistent random benchmark that resamples scores using blocks of length $h$. This ensures models are not rewarded for spurious correlations arising from overlapping targets.
4. **Multiple-Testing Control**: We evaluate 69 distinct model-horizon hypotheses. We apply the step-down Holm-Bonferroni correction to verify whether apparent signals survive family-wise error adjustments.
5. **Isolated Holdout**: The recent 2024–2025 market recovery period is strictly reserved as an out-of-sample holdout. It was never accessed during model tuning or threshold choice.

## 8. Consistent Leaders Definition & Mechanics

- **Definition**: A company is classified as a **Consistent Leader** on date $t$ if and only if:
  1. It belongs to the **High Upside** tier in at least **two** of the three horizons (Short, Mid, Long).
  2. It belongs to the **Low Upside** tier in **none** of the three horizons.
  3. It does **not** carry an active liquidity warning flag (`zero_trade_share_63d < threshold`).
- **Empirical Validation**: Historically across development walk-forward periods, Consistent Leaders achieved an outperformance win rate of **51.7%** on Mid horizon (vs 48.4% for other stocks) and **56.9%** on Long horizon (vs 46.4% for others).

## 9. Key Limitations & Risk Disclosures

Investors and analysts should account for the following structural constraints:
1. **Survivorship Bias**: The index constituent roster represents current members of the S&P SL20. Companies that went bankrupt, merged, or were delisted over the historical 2001–2025 backtest window are not included.
2. **Single Holdout Regime**: The holdout period (2024–2025) reflects a sharp post-debt-restructuring relief rally. Performance under stagflation or severe currency depreciation regimes may differ.
3. **Frontier Market Liquidity & Execution Frictions**: While our backtests model 100 bps per side (200 bps round-trip friction), actual execution in thinly traded Colombo stocks can experience severe price impact.
4. **No Fundamental or News Signals**: The engine processes price and volume action only. Sudden corporate disclosures or credit rating revisions will not appear in scores until reflected in market quotes.
5. **Data Update Latency**: End-of-day scores update after daily exchange trade processing is completed.
